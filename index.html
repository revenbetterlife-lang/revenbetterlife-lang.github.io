About me

A student who learns by shipping.

I'm a second-year Data Science student (Class of 2028) at Al-Manoufia National University. I care about the practical side of AI: not just models, but the workflows around them that actually save people time.

Most of my work lives in two places: Python, where I analyze data and build OOP-based systems, and n8n, where I design AI automations that connect tools like Gemini, Telegram, WhatsApp, and Odoo into working products.
